A note, minus the teasing (mostly)

What I want you to know.

Not everything fits into a joke or a birthday caption. So here's the honest version, in my very best handwriting font.

Hey Apoorva,

Happy birthday, chudail. Aaj ka din tumhara hai: favourite people, kuch delicious, and enough cake to make the candle situation slightly concerning.

Humari nok-jhok, ek dusre ko irritate karna, beech-beech mein deep talks—these are all parts of a friendship I genuinely value. I’m really glad I get to call you my friend.

Meri baaton ya actions se kabhi tum hurt hui ho, I’m genuinely sorry. Meri intention jo bhi thi, usse tum par kya asar hua woh kam nahi hota. I want to listen better and be more thoughtful.

Is apology ke saath koi expectation nahi hai—tumhe forgive karna, reply karna, ya kuch bhi explain karna bilkul zaroori nahi. I just wanted to acknowledge it sincerely.

For the year ahead, I hope you find more peace, confidence, self-love, and plenty of reasons to be happy. May acting bring you roles you’re excited about, and may your hard work open doors to the future success you want. Khud pe bharosa rakhna—your story is yours to write.

With lots of friendship (and a tiny bit of teasing),
— Agrim
