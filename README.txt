APOORVA BIRTHDAY SURPRISE
=========================

Files:
- index.html
- assets/ (the selected photo memories)

Preview:
1. Keep the folder structure intact.
2. Double-click index.html to open the invitation in Chrome, or serve this folder locally and open the project URL.
3. On your computer, select "Open Your Surprise" to preview the full scrapbook before the birthday. The public site remains locked until the birthday.
4. The countdown and birthday unlock use 5 November 2026 at 12:00 AM India time (IST).
5. The page attempts to start "Aww Tera Happy Budday" as it loads, using the full song on Zee Music Company's official YouTube channel. The compact music control has play/pause, mute, and volume controls; YouTube needs an internet connection.
6. Browsers often block autoplay with sound. If that happens, the first click, tap, or keyboard interaction anywhere on the page retries playback; no separate music-button click is needed. The song stays in one player while visitors move through the scrapbook.
7. The balloon game uses a bundled, short WAV pop sound, so its sound effect does not depend on the YouTube player or an external audio service.
8. All eight supplied photos are used in the scrapbook with individual Hinglish captions. The gallery filters solo and shared photos, and the compliment notes open when tapped.

Publish with Netlify:
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the whole `apoorva_birthday` folder (or its ZIP) into the upload area.
3. Netlify will provide a public website link.
4. Send that link on WhatsApp.

You can edit the text in index.html. Do not rename the assets folder or the memory image files.
